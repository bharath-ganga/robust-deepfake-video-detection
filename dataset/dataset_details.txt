Datasets Used

1. FaceForensics++
   - Used for training and evaluation of deepfake detection model
   - Contains real and manipulated facial videos

2. DeepFake Detection Challenge (DFDC)
   - Large-scale dataset containing deepfake videos
   - Used for robustness evaluation

3. Celeb-DF
   - High-quality deepfake video dataset
   - Used for testing model generalization

Dataset Type:
- Video-based deepfake datasets

Preprocessing Steps:
- Video frame extraction
- Face detection
- Face cropping and resizing
- Image normalization
